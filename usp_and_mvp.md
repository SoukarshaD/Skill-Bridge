# SkillBridge / Setu-Kaushal (SIH26134)

## 🌟 Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

**"Turn labour-market signals into a living, evidence-based training plan — automatically, continuously, at district granularity."**

Unlike traditional portals that act as static job boards or course catalogs, SkillBridge's USP is its dynamic **LMI (Labour-Market Intelligence) Engine**. It creates a continuous, automated feedback loop between what industry employers *currently* need and what academic/government institutions are *actually* teaching. It flags obsolete courses in real-time and generates hyper-local (district-level) plans to ensure every training rupee is spent on skills that guarantee employment.

---

## 🚀 Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope

The MVP focuses entirely on proving this intelligence loop works. It includes the following **P0 Core Features**:

1. **Demand-Signal Ingestion:** 
   A system that ingests and aggregates real-world data from job postings (scraped/API) and structured employer surveys.

2. **Skill-Gap Engine:** 
   The core algorithm that calculates the gap between demand and supply, categorized by specific roles, skills, geographic locations, and proficiency levels.

3. **Course-to-Skill Mapping:** 
   The ability to map university/training center programs against a standardized skill catalog (NSQF-aligned).

4. **Obsolescence / Oversupply Flagging:** 
   Automated logic that flags courses teaching outdated or oversupplied skills, complete with an explainable rationale for why it was flagged.

5. **District-Level Training Plans:** 
   An automated, exportable report generator for Scheme Admins to see exactly which skills need funding in which specific districts.

6. **Employer Validation Workflow (P1/MVP-Adjacent):** 
   A stubbed workflow allowing employers to review, comment on, and endorse proposed academic curriculum changes.
